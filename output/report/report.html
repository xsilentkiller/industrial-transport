Reading notes

  • The convergence chart is the PSO loss curve: lower is better.
  • The timeline assumes trips are performed sequentially in solution order; it is a workload view, not a clock schedule.
  • The route map uses the latitude/longitude in locations.csv.
  • The solution is a heuristic result, not a certified global optimum.
