Conclusion

  • Lowest median gap: Current (random-key memetic PSO) (1.98 %, median real cost 1,408.46 EUR).
  • Largest share of the improvement made by the swarm itself: B: discrete PSO (path relinking) (91 %).
  • Current (random-key memetic PSO): freezing the swarm changes the mean objective by +13.91 EUR (the swarm helps).
  • B: discrete PSO (path relinking): freezing the swarm changes the mean objective by +57.14 EUR (the swarm helps).
  • C: cluster-centre PSO: freezing the swarm changes the mean objective by +20.78 EUR (the swarm helps).

Fair by construction: every method plans the same 250-customer days with the same cost model and the instructor's rules, gets the same number of plan evaluations inside its loop, and is judged on days that were never used to choose its settings. The current method runs with today's defaults (it alone has local search inside the loop); B and C have none, so their improvement comes from the swarm and the final polish.
